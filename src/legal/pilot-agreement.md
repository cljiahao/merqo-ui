## Data during the pilot

During the Pilot Term, a Pilot Kit processes the Pilot Vendor's real
end-customer data — including phone numbers, order or booking history,
and Telegram links — as live production data, handled in accordance with
Merqo's Privacy Policy. Using a Pilot Kit during the Pilot Term places no
obligation on Merqo to launch that Pilot Kit generally, and no obligation
on the Pilot Vendor to convert to a paid plan at the end of the Pilot
Term.

## Wind-down

If the Pilot Term ends without the Pilot Vendor converting to the
standard terms, or if Merqo decides not to take a Pilot Kit to general
availability, Merqo will: give the Pilot Vendor at least 30 days' written
notice before the Pilot Vendor's access ends; provide the Pilot Vendor a
full export of its data from the Pilot Kit in a usable format; then
delete that data within 30 days of the export being provided, or within
30 days of the notice period ending if the Pilot Vendor does not request
an export, and confirm deletion to the Pilot Vendor in writing. Each
party's confidential information held by the other is returned or
destroyed at the same time.

## Converting to the standard terms

Before the Pilot Term ends, Merqo will present the Pilot Vendor with the
then-current Merqo Vendor Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Continuing
to use a Pilot Kit past the end of the Pilot Term requires the Pilot
Vendor's affirmative acceptance of those terms, through the same
acceptance gate that applies to any other vendor. If the Pilot Vendor
declines to accept those terms, its account is suspended at the end of
the Pilot Term and the export and deletion process described in
Wind-down applies in the same way.
