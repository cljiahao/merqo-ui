# paykit Schedule

This Schedule supplements the Merqo Vendor Terms of Service and applies
only to a Vendor's use of paykit. Terms defined in the Terms of Service
have the same meaning here. Where this Schedule conflicts with the Terms
of Service in respect of paykit, this Schedule controls.

## paykit is not a payment service under the Payment Services Act 2019

paykit is a deposit- and payment-**tracking** tool. Merqo is not a
payment service provider under Singapore's Payment Services Act 2019
("PSA"), and paykit is not designed, built, or operated to bring Merqo
within any regulated payment service the PSA defines — including account
issuance, domestic or cross-border money transfer, merchant acquisition,
e-money issuance, or digital payment token services.

In particular, and going beyond the general observation that Merqo never
holds a Vendor's or end customer's funds:

- **Merqo is never a party to any contractual agreement under which a
  payment transaction is accepted or processed.** The Vendor's payment
  service provider, acquiring bank, or payment gateway is a party to that
  agreement with the Vendor; Merqo is not, and paykit does not insert
  Merqo into that contractual chain in any transaction.
- **Merqo never determines, initiates, authorises, or settles a transfer
  of funds.** A payment is initiated and authorised between the Vendor's
  end customer and the Vendor's own payment service provider or bank,
  entirely outside paykit. paykit has no technical capability to trigger,
  approve, or reverse a fund movement.
- **paykit provides display, record-keeping, and messaging services
  only.** paykit lets a Vendor record that a deposit or payment is
  expected, mark it as received against information the Vendor enters or
  reconciles, and notify an end customer of a payment status. This is a
  record of a payment event the Vendor tells paykit occurred; it is not
  the mechanism by which the payment itself is accepted or moves.
- Where paykit displays a QR code, payment link, or bank reference
  supplied by the Vendor's own payment service provider, Merqo merely
  displays what the Vendor configured; Merqo does not generate, control,
  or process the underlying payment rail.

Because Merqo is never a party to the payment transaction and never
determines, initiates, authorises, or settles the transfer of funds, we
consider paykit's activity to fall outside the PSA's definition of a
payment service and, in the alternative, within the PSA's technical
service provider exclusion for a service limited to processing and
storage of data, or a messaging service, in relation to a payment
transaction. This position may need to be revisited if paykit's features
change; this Schedule will be updated if that happens.

## The Vendor's own payment service provider

The Vendor's relationship with its own payment service provider, bank,
or payment gateway — including that provider's fees, settlement times,
chargebacks, and compliance obligations — is the Vendor's alone. Merqo is
not a party to it, has no visibility into its terms, and takes no
responsibility for that provider's acts or omissions. The Vendor is
solely responsible for holding any licence, registration, or exemption
that provider or Singapore law requires for the Vendor's own acceptance
of payments.

## Deposits and refunds

Where a Vendor uses paykit to track deposits, the Vendor — not Merqo —
determines the deposit amount, the conditions for forfeiting or refunding
it, and whether and when a refund is due. paykit records what the Vendor
configures and reports; it does not adjudicate a deposit or refund
dispute between the Vendor and an end customer.
